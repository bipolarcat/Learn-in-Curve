## Section: where the money actually goes

**Eyebrow:** THE £9.99, EXPLAINED

**Body:**

The PMQ in 5 Days course — every learning objective, every quiz, the full mock exam question bank — is free. It always will be.

The only payment on the platform is a one-off £9.99 that unlocks the premium bundle: AI tutor (Sly), an extra quiz-generation option, AI grading on a full mock exam, and a complete additional mock exam.

That £9.99 isn't a markup. It's a platform fee — it covers the actual infrastructure cost of running the AI tools, with a £5 fair-usage allowance for the AI tutor built in. Use more than that and you can top up on a pay-as-you-go basis. No hidden limits, no silent auto-upgrade to a subscription. You can always see how much keeps the platform running and how much you get to spend.

*(Note: fair-usage credit at unlock is £5 of the £9.99; top-ups credit 70% to the metre.)*

## Notes on what I deliberately left out

- **No claim that the founder personally holds the APM PMQ certification.** His LinkedIn lists 6+ years of PM delivery experience, a construction PM master's from Heriot-Watt, and several shorter certifications (Project Resource Management, Project Portfolio Management Foundations, a ChatGPT-for-PMs course) — but not a PMQ pass. Since the whole brief is "trust and transparency," I didn't want to imply a credential that isn't confirmed. If you do hold the PMQ, tell me and I'll fold it in — it's a strong, honest addition. If not, the current wording ("the kind of pressure PMQ actually tests you on," from real delivery work) holds up without it.
- **No specific fair-usage quota** — used the word "fair usage" only, as instructed, flagged inline above so whoever fills in the number later doesn't miss it.
- **No stats beyond the 98-learner figure**, which you confirmed. Framed modestly ("currently used by," not "trusted by thousands") — matches the transparency vision; inflating a small early number would work against it.
