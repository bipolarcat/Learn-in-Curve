**1. Which team development model shows a number of roles that are optimal for an effective team?**
A) Tuckman
B) Hackman
C) Belbin
D) Margerison McCann
**Correct Answer:** C) Belbin

A) Plant
B) Shaper
C) Implementer
D) Specialist
**Correct Answer:** A) Plant

A) Co-coordinator

**Correct Answer:** C) Resource-Investigator
