**Question 1:** What is the primary purpose of a business case?
A) To design the project deliverables.
B) To act as the justification for the initiation, investment, and/or continuation of a project.
C) To act as a substitute for the project management plan.
D) To identify all project stakeholders.
**Answer:** **B**.

**Question 2:** A business case evaluates alternative options based on which three key criteria?
A) Time, Quality, Scope.
B) Benefits, Costs, Risks.
C) Resources, Dependencies, Constraints.
D) Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities.
**Answer:** **B**.

D) Economic analysis.
**Answer:** **A**.
