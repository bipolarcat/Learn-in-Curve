**Question 1:** Whose leadership model emphasizes addressing team performance by identifying which level of need a team member is seeking to address?
A) Herzberg
B) Maslow
C) McGregor
D) Vroom
**Answer:** B) Maslow

**Question 2:** According to Maslow's theory, what happens once a specific need is satisfied?
A) It becomes a hygiene factor.
B) It continues to motivate the individual indefinitely.
C) It ceases to continue to motivate.
D) The individual skips the next level.
**Answer:** C) It ceases to continue to motivate.

**Question 3:** What forms the base level of Maslow's standard hierarchy of needs?
A) Safety and security
B) Self-esteem
C) Physiological needs
D) Love and belonging
**Answer:** C) Physiological needs

**Question 4:** In the "Project Hierarchy of Needs" model, what represents the highest level of achievement (equivalent to self-actualization)?
A) Success
B) Recognition
C) Teamwork
D) Facilities, welfare, infrastructure
**Answer:** A) Success

**Question 5:** Which of the following is considered a 'hygiene factor' in Herzberg's Two-Factor Theory?
A) Achievement
B) Responsibility
C) Recognition
D) Salary
**Answer:** D) Salary

**Question 6:** According to Herzberg's theory, what is the primary consequence of an absence of hygiene factors?
A) It leads to job dissatisfaction.
B) It leads to job satisfaction.
C) It increases motivation.
D) It empowers employees.
**Answer:** A) It leads to job dissatisfaction.

**Question 7:** In Herzberg's theory, what type of factor provides opportunities for tangible results and recognizing individuals when they achieve goals?
A) Hygiene factors
B) Motivators
C) Physiological factors
D) Intrapersonal factors
**Answer:** B) Motivators

**Question 8:** Which of the following is classified as a 'Motivator' rather than a 'Hygiene Factor' by Herzberg?
A) Working conditions
B) Company Policy
C) Advancement
D) Supervision
**Answer:** C) Advancement

**Question 9:** McGregor's Theory X assumes that the typical worker has which of the following characteristics?
A) Self-motivated and creative.
B) Enjoys work and takes pride in what they do.
C) Dislikes work and is naturally unmotivated to work.
D) Embraces democratic environments.
**Answer:** C) Dislikes work and is naturally unmotivated to work.

**Question 10:** What type of management style is typically adopted by a Theory X manager?
A) Empowering and delegative.
B) Focuses on heightened supervision and financial rewards.
C) Democratic and creative.
D) Coaching and mentoring.
**Answer:** B) Focuses on heightened supervision and financial rewards.

**Question 11:** According to McGregor, what kind of worker is self-motivated, creative, and takes pride in what they do?

**Answer:** B) Theory Y worker

**Question 12:** How does a Theory Y manager typically approach their team?

C) By creating a democratic environment that encompasses creativity and discussion.
D) By assuming workers lack ambition.
**Answer:** C) By creating a democratic environment that encompasses creativity and discussion.

**Question 13:** What is a potential risk if a Theory Y manager leads workers who conform more to Theory X?
