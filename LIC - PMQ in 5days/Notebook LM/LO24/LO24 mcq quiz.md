**1. What does change control systematically manage?**
A) Routine business-as-usual operations
B) Alterations to project scope, timelines, or resources
C) The initial project funding
D) Team conflicts
**Answer: B**

**2. What can be the consequence of unmanaged change within a project environment?**
A) It can have far-reaching impacts on the project and business-as-usual activities.
B) It strictly benefits the business case.
C) It automatically transitions the project to an iterative lifecycle.
D) It eliminates the need for risk management.
**Answer: A**

**3. What is a "change freeze"?**
A) A pause in project funding.
B) A period where the project team takes mandatory leave.
C) A mechanism that prevents any further change from being considered to protect project delivery.
D) A delay in the project schedule.
**Answer: C**

**4. In an iterative life cycle, how is change primarily managed?**

C) By automatically deferring all requests to the end of the project.
