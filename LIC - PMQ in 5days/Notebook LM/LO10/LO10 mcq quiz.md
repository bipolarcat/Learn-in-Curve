Here are 50 multiple-choice questions based on the provided sources regarding stakeholder engagement and communication management. 

**1. Stakeholder analysis enables project managers to identify:**
A) Only external dependencies
B) The project’s key stakeholders, their interests, and how those interests affect riskiness
C) The exact cost of the project
D) The technical skill requirements of the project team
**Correct Answer: B.** Stakeholder analysis enables project managers to identify a project’s key stakeholders, their interests in the project and the ways in which those interests affect project riskiness and viability.

**2. By identifying the views of stakeholders towards the project, stakeholder analysis primarily supports:**
A) Procurement processes
B) Stakeholder influence and engagement
C) Setting the final project budget
D) Defining the technical architecture
**Correct Answer: B.** Identifying the views of stakeholders towards the project supports stakeholder influence and engagement.

**3. Which two factors are most commonly assessed in a grid to determine stakeholder engagement strategies?**
A) Power and Interest
B) Cost and Time
C) Technical skill and Availability
D) Physical location and Language
**Correct Answer: A.** Stakeholders are assessed on a grid based on their level of power or influence and their level of interest.

**4. Why is it considered impossible to please every stakeholder on a project?**
A) Because budgets are always strictly limited
B) Because project timelines are usually too short
C) Because stakeholders will have conflicting interests
D) Because communication channels are often restricted
**Correct Answer: C.** It is impossible to please everyone because stakeholders will often have conflicting interests.

**5. What should be the primary aim when dealing with stakeholders who have conflicting interests?**
A) Ignore the conflicting interests to save time
B) Only satisfy the sponsor
C) Ensure the highest possible satisfaction among stakeholders is achieved
D) Escalate all conflicts to senior management immediately
**Correct Answer: C.** The aim should be to ensure the highest possible satisfaction among stakeholders is achieved.

**6. A stakeholder with high power, high interest, and a negative attitude towards the project requires:**
A) Little to no engagement
B) Intensive engagement from the project manager or sponsor
C) Only a monthly newsletter
D) Only engagement regarding technical jargon
**Correct Answer: B.** A stakeholder identified as having high power, high interest, and a negative attitude may need intensive engagement to avoid any risk they might pose.

**7. What is the recommended strategy for a stakeholder with high power, high interest, and a positive attitude?**
A) Give them little engagement
B) Regular engagement to maintain their support and commitment to champion the project
C) Provide them with glossaries to improve technical skills
D) Isolate them from other stakeholders
**Correct Answer: B.** They may need regular engagement to maintain their support and commitment to the project so that they can continue to champion the project.

**8. What might happen if there is a lack of communication with low power, high interest stakeholders who have a positive attitude?**
A) It can cause concerns leading to negativity towards the project
B) They will naturally become high power stakeholders
C) They will take over the project management role
D) The project budget will automatically decrease
**Correct Answer: A.** Miscommunication or lack of communication can cause concerns within stakeholder groups which could lead to negativity towards the project.

**9. Which of the following is a classic example of a stakeholder with high power, low interest, and a neutral attitude?**
A) The primary project sponsor
B) A regulatory body such as the Health and Safety Executive
C) A community member living miles away from the project
D) A heavily invested business owner
**Correct Answer: B.** A regulatory body such as the Health and Safety Executive typically falls into this category, where providing specific information is a regulatory requirement.

**10. How should a project manager engage with a low power, low interest stakeholder who has a neutral attitude?**
A) Provide intensive daily face-to-face meetings
B) Exclude them from all communications
C) Provide little engagement, such as just a newsletter or similar
D) Assign them a senior management sponsor
**Correct Answer: C.** They are likely to need little engagement, and the provision of some information can be just a newsletter or similar.

**11. According to the power/interest grid principles, why is it beneficial to give low power, low interest stakeholders minimal engagement?**
A) It prevents them from forming alliances
B) It frees up resources to focus on engagement with others with more power and interest
C) It ensures they do not understand the project scope
D) It violates governance policies but saves money
**Correct Answer: B.** Limiting engagement for this group frees up resources to focus on engagement with others with more power and interest.
