Here are 50 multiple choice questions based on the provided material regarding Resource Management (Learning Objective 21):

**21a) Determining Resource Requirements**

**1. What is the primary focus of resource management?**
A) Controlling external suppliers only
B) Effective allocation, utilisation, and monitoring of resources throughout the project life cycle
C) Managing the business as usual (BAU) operations
D) Ensuring all project tasks are completed sequentially
**Answer:** The correct option is B, as resource management focuses on the effective allocation, utilisation, and monitoring of resources throughout the project life cycle.

**2. Which of the following is an important consideration when establishing resource requirements?**
A) The personal preferences of resources
B) The needs of the project versus the needs of business as usual (BAU)
C) The marketing strategy of the host organisation
D) Identifying all optional activities
**Answer:** The correct option is B, because establishing resource requirements requires an understanding of the needs of the project versus the needs of business as usual (BAU).

**3. How can resources place constraints on project logistics?**
A) By limiting the scope of the project objectives
B) Through the space needed to operate and store things
C) By changing the sequence of all activities
D) By dictating the business case
**Answer:** The correct option is B, because resources may constrain project logistics through the space needed to operate and store things.

**4. How might resources drive the pace of a project?**
A) By determining the communication plan
B) By speeding up the approval of the business case
C) By placing constraints on what can be done and when
D) By increasing the project's overall budget
**Answer:** The correct option is C, as resources may drive project pace by placing constraints on what can be done and when.

**5. Who does a project manager typically work with to secure resources at the required times for the schedule?**
A) Only external suppliers
B) The project sponsor solely
C) Resource or BAU managers
D) The assurance team
**Answer:** The correct option is C, because project managers work with resource or BAU managers to secure resources at the required times for the schedule.

**6. If a resource has come from a supplier, who might be allocated to manage relations?**
A) The team leader
B) An external resource manager
C) The internal HR department
D) The quality manager
**Answer:** The correct option is B, because if the resource has come from a supplier, an external resource manager may be allocated to manage relations.

**7. Which of the following is NOT listed as a critical consideration when planning project resources?**
A) Skillsets and capabilities of resources
B) Personal preferences of resources
C) Types of resources needed to support activities
D) The selected lifecycle approach
**Answer:** The correct option is B, as the personal preferences of resources are not critical to take into account, while skillsets, resource types, and lifecycle approaches are key considerations.

**8. Who might provide line management to resources operating in Business as Usual (BAU)?**
A) External stakeholders
B) Resource or BAU managers
C) The project sponsor
D) The project manager directly
**Answer:** The correct option is B, because resource or BAU managers may provide line management to the resources in BAU.

**9. Identifying and scheduling required resources is important because it ensures resources are allocated effectively, which helps to avoid what?**
A) Having to write a business case
B) Bottlenecks and clashes
C) The need for an organisational breakdown structure
D) Changes to the project scope
**Answer:** The correct option is B, because identifying and scheduling resources ensures they are allocated effectively, avoiding bottlenecks and clashes.

**10. Effective resource scheduling also helps to manage risk surrounding what?**
A) Resource gaps or conflicts
B) Changes in market trends
C) Competitor actions
D) Selecting the wrong life cycle
**Answer:** The correct option is A, as identifying and scheduling resources manages risk around any resource gaps or conflicts.

**21b) Organisational Breakdown Structure (OBS) and RACI Matrix**

**11. What does an Organisational Breakdown Structure (OBS) detail?**
A) The complete schedule of the project tasks
B) The organisation for the project and the resources available
C) The cost of all resources needed
D) The external constraints of the project
**Answer:** The correct option is B, because an organisational breakdown structure details the organisation for the project and the resources available.

**12. How does an OBS assist with resource problems?**
A) It automatically resolves them
B) It assigns a new budget to them
C) It makes initial escalation routes clear
D) It reassigns the tasks to other departments
**Answer:** The correct option is C, as an OBS makes initial escalation routes clear, which helps with any resource problems that may need escalating.

**13. What is created when a project manager decides if team members are responsible, accountable, consulted, or informed for each task?**
A) A project schedule
B) A cost breakdown structure
C) A responsibility assignment matrix (RAM)
D) A resource smoothing chart
**Answer:** The correct option is C, because deciding if a team member is responsible, accountable, consulted, or informed creates a responsibility assignment matrix (RAM).

**14. In a RACI matrix, what does "Responsible" mean?**
A) Approves the works and is held accountable
B) Does the work and owns any problems
C) Included in decision making
D) Kept up-to-date with progress
**Answer:** The correct option is B, because "Responsible" means the person does the work and owns any problems.

**15. In a RACI matrix, what does "Accountable" mean?**
A) Does the work and owns any problems
B) Approves the works and is held accountable for the deliveries
C) Provides a key supportive role
D) Kept up-to-date with results
**Answer:** The correct option is B, because "Accountable" refers to the person who approves the works and is held accountable for the deliveries.

**16. In a RACI matrix, what does "Consulted" mean?**
A) Approves the deliveries
B) Does the work and owns problems
C) Included in decision making and provides a key supportive role
D) Only kept up-to-date with progress
**Answer:** The correct option is C, as "Consulted" refers to being included in decision making and providing a key supportive role.

**17. In a RACI matrix, what does "Informed" mean?**
A) Kept up-to-date with progress and results
B) Approves the works
C) Provides a supportive role in decision making
D) Does the physical labor
**Answer:** The correct option is A, because "Informed" means being kept up-to-date with progress and results.

**18. What is considered best practice when assigning roles in a RACI matrix?**
A) Multiple people should be accountable to share risk
B) One person to be responsible and one person to be accountable
C) Everyone should be consulted on every task
D) The project manager should be accountable for all tasks
**Answer:** The correct option is B, because it is best practice for one person to be responsible and one person to be accountable.

**19. Why should resource or BAU managers consult the RAM?**
A) To find out the project's budget
B) To check if the project is delayed
C) To understand and agree the work that has been allocated to their people
D) To change the project scope
**Answer:** The correct option is C, because resource managers should consult the RAM/RACI to understand and agree the work that has been allocated to their people.

**20. What serves as a communication device to ensure people are aware of the work they must do and their position in the project organisation?**
A) The project business case
B) The RAM or RACI
C) The project baseline
D) The risk register
**Answer:** The correct option is B, as the RAM or RACI is a communication device to ensure that the people involved are aware of the work they have to do and their position.

**21. In summary, a RAM provides an understanding of specific responsibilities and what else?**
A) The final cost of the project
B) The exact duration of each task
C) The level of accountability or contribution expected from named roles
D) The quality specifications for materials
**Answer:** The correct option is C, because the RAM provides an understanding of specific responsibilities and the level of accountability or contribution expected from named roles or individuals.

**22. If a project manager sets up a workshop to complete a RACI, which structures are typically used together to clarify roles against tasks?**
A) The risk register and the issue log
B) The budget and the schedule
C) The Organisational Breakdown Structure (OBS) and Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)

**23. How does the use of a RAM specifically streamline resource management?**

B) By eliminating the need for a schedule

**Answer:** The correct option is C, as using a RAM supports resource management by supporting effective task delegation to resources.

**24. In the RACI matrix, the role that acts as a "point of reference for the project team" is supported by which document?**
A) The RAM
B) The WBS only
C) The critical path network
D) The cost estimate

**25. Tasks defined in an Organisational Breakdown Structure help to do what for the people involved?**

C) Reduce the project duration

**21c) Categorising and Allocating Resources (Linear and Iterative)**

**26. Resources are typically categorised into which four types?**
A) Time, budget, scope, and quality
B) Human, materials, plant or equipment, and facilities
C) Internal, external, local, and national
D) Software, hardware, personnel, and data
**Answer:** The correct option is B, as resources are categorised into four types: human, materials, plant or equipment, and facilities.

**27. Which of the following is given as an example of a "facilities" resource?**
A) A project manager
B) Bricks and steel
C) Specialist test sites
D) A budget line item
**Answer:** The correct option is C, because "facilities" resources are listed as things like specialist test sites.

**28. Resources can be defined as what types of items needed to deliver project objectives?**
A) Only internal corporate items
B) Labour and non-labour items
C) Solely financial items
D) Intangible digital items
**Answer:** The correct option is B, because resources are defined as the labour and non-labour items needed to deliver the project objectives successfully.

**29. What must a project manager consider when looking to allocate resources?**
A) Only the cost of materials
B) The allocation of resource type, quantity needed, availability, and cost
C) The project sponsor's daily schedule
D) Only whether the resource is external
**Answer:** The correct option is B, because when allocating resources, the project manager must consider the allocation of resource type, how many are needed, availability, and cost.

**30. When using a linear life cycle, what planning assumption is made when creating the schedule?**
A) All resources will constantly change
B) All resources will be available when required
C) Resources will only be available on weekends
D) No external resources will be used
**Answer:** The correct option is B, because when using a linear life cycle, a planning assumption is made that all resources will be available when required.

**31. If resources are not available when required in a linear life cycle, what can be applied to try and fit the work in?**
A) Resource management techniques
B) Quality reduction protocols
C) Complete scope cancellation
D) Ignoring the schedule entirely
**Answer:** The correct option is A, as resource management techniques can be applied to try and fit the work in with the resources available.

**32. For iterative life cycles, requirements are prioritised and delivered within a timebox using what kind of resources?**
A) Unlimited external resources
B) Pre-allocated resources available
C) Resources borrowed from other companies
D) Only uncertified resources
**Answer:** The correct option is B, because for iterative life cycles, requirements are delivered within the timebox using the pre-allocated resources available.

**33. In an iterative life cycle, if all scope must be achieved to the agreed quality, what may be needed?**
A) A complete change of project sponsor
B) An extension of time
C) A reduction in project benefits
D) The elimination of the OBS
**Answer:** The correct option is B, because if all scope must be achieved to the agreed quality in an iterative life cycle, an extension of time will be needed.

**34. Which of the following is a project manager NOT required to consider when allocating resources in an iterative lifecycle?**

B) Who will be committing resources
C) Whether the required resource is on the critical path
D) Whether all resources are available for the entirety of the project
**Answer:** The correct option is D, as whether all resources are available for the entirety of the project is a linear lifecycle assumption and not a strict consideration for iterative lifecycles.

**35. Why can resource allocation be challenging for iterative life cycle schedules?**

**36. Using pre-allocated resources in an iterative life cycle could potentially lead to what issue?**

**37. In contrast to iterative life cycles, linear life cycles require what to determine resources needed?**

**38. In an iterative life cycle timebox, if resources cannot meet the full requirement, what is typically varied?**

**21d) Resource Smoothing and Resource Levelling**

**39. Which resource management technique is used when time is more important than cost?**
A) Resource levelling
B) Resource allocating
C) Resource smoothing
D) Resource scheduling
**Answer:** The correct option is C, because resource smoothing is used when time is more important than cost.

**40. Which technique is used where a fixed amount of resource is available, and the end date of the project can be delayed?**
A) Resource smoothing
B) Resource levelling
C) Fast tracking
D) Crashing
**Answer:** The correct option is B, because resource levelling is used where a fixed amount of resource is available, and the end date of the project can be delayed.

**41. What specific question does resource smoothing attempt to answer?**
A) "With the resources available, when will the work be finished?"
B) "How much float does the critical path have?"
C) "What resources do I need to deliver the work within the fixed timescale?"
D) "Who is accountable for the delays?"
**Answer:** The correct option is C, because resource smoothing answers the question, "What resources do I need to deliver the work within the fixed timescale?".

**42. What specific question does resource levelling attempt to answer?**
A) "With the resources available, when will the work be finished?"
B) "What is the cost of extending the deadline?"
C) "What resources do I need to deliver the work within the fixed timescale?"
D) "How can we change the critical path entirely?"
**Answer:** The correct option is A, because resource levelling answers the question, "With the resources available, when will the work be finished?".

**43. How does resource smoothing protect the end date of the project?**
A) By ensuring resource usage avoids peaks and troughs without touching critical path activities
B) By extending the deadline indefinitely
C) By dropping half of the project deliverables
D) By working solely on the critical path
**Answer:** The correct option is A, as smoothing avoids peaks and troughs of resource demand and is limited to activities with available float, not affecting the critical path.

**44. Which of the following is a method to achieve resource levelling?**
A) Reducing the duration of all tasks
B) Increasing task durations or moving activities to a different time
C) Making the schedule entirely linear without constraints
D) Refusing to redefine scope or specifications
**Answer:** The correct option is B, because levelling can be achieved through increasing task durations or moving non-critical (and even critical path) activities to a different time.

**45. What happens to the critical path during resource levelling?**
A) It is completely unaffected
B) It may change, as task adjustments can affect it
C) It is deleted from the schedule
D) It becomes exactly equal to the float
**Answer:** The correct option is B, because in resource levelling, the critical path may change, as task adjustments can affect it.

**46. What happens to the critical path during resource smoothing?**
A) It is completely completely overhauled
B) It may change depending on the resources available
C) It is not affected, as adjustments are within float limits
D) It becomes the sole focus of all resource managers
**Answer:** The correct option is C, because in resource smoothing, the critical path is not affected, as adjustments are within float limits.

**47. What is the impact on a project's schedule when utilizing resource smoothing?**
A) Medium to High, as the project end date can change
B) Low, as the project end date is not changed
C) Very high, because tasks are removed
D) Unknown until the project finishes
**Answer:** The correct option is B, because the schedule impact of resource smoothing is low, as the project end date is not changed.

**48. In resource management, what is the primary focus of resource levelling?**
A) Maintaining a consistent level of resource usage
B) Maximising the peaks and troughs of demand
C) Ensuring resource constraints are not violated
D) Ensuring the project finishes ahead of schedule
**Answer:** The correct option is C, because the primary focus of resource levelling is ensuring resource constraints are not violated.

**49. The overall aim of both resource smoothing and levelling is to optimise resources through the flow of work to prevent what?**
A) The need to change the planned end date of the project
B) The need to pay resource managers
C) The usage of external materials
D) Having to create a RACI matrix
**Answer:** The correct option is A, as the overall aim of the techniques is to optimise resources, preventing the need to change the planned end date of the project.

**50. If resources (people or equipment) are completely finite and optimisation techniques cannot resolve the constraints, what may be the only option?**
A) Complete the project instantly
B) Extend project timelines
C) Abandon the organisational breakdown structure
D) Ignore quality requirements entirely
**Answer:** The correct option is B, because if resources are finite, there may be no option but to extend project timelines.
