**1. What action increases the likelihood of project outputs being accepted?**
A) Limiting stakeholder involvement to the final stages.
B) Managing stakeholder expectations from the outset.
C) Reducing the overall project budget.
D) Transferring risk to the end users as quickly as possible.
**Correct Answer:** B) Managing stakeholder expectations from the outset.

**2. Who should be identified and engaged with as key stakeholders throughout the project to support a successful transition?**
A) Competitors and external market regulators.
B) End users and project sponsors.
C) Third-party auditing firms.
D) Only the core project team.
**Correct Answer:** B) End users and project sponsors.

**3. How can a project manager best set the path for handover and acceptance early on?**
A) By familiarising key stakeholders with the deliverables from the outset.
B) By avoiding communication until deliverables are completely finished.
C) By signing acceptance certificates before the project begins.
D) By increasing the scope of the project deliverables midway.
**Correct Answer:** A) By familiarising key stakeholders with the deliverables from the outset.

**4. What reduces the risk of the project manager or end users misunderstanding the requirements of the deliverables?**
A) Detailed budgeting.
B) Clear communication.
C) Extended life cycles.
D) Strict penalties.
**Correct Answer:** B) Clear communication.

**5. When should preparing for the new business-as-usual be considered?**
A) Only after all project deliverables are completed.
B) Early in the project life cycle.
C) Exclusively during the transition phase.
D) After the benefits have been fully realised.
**Correct Answer:** B) Early in the project life cycle.

**6. Why should business readiness be considered a continuous activity throughout the project?**
A) To ensure intended users have bought into the outcomes.
B) To continually re-evaluate the project budget.
C) To delay the deployment phase.
D) To find reasons to extend the project timeline.
**Correct Answer:** A) To ensure intended users have bought into the outcomes.

**7. Who typically carries out business readiness activities?**
A) The project sponsor.
B) External consultants.
C) The project team.
D) The end users.
**Correct Answer:** C) The project team.

**8. What helps the project team identify, empower, and motivate champions for the 'new normal'?**
A) Proactively engaging with people to understand business-as-usual processes.
B) Implementing strict new rules without consultation.
C) Deflecting all stakeholder inquiries to the sponsor.
D) Focusing purely on the financial bottom line.
**Correct Answer:** A) Proactively engaging with people to understand business-as-usual processes.

**9. What method helps build an understanding of the organisational impacts on people, performance, processes, systems, and culture?**
A) Financial cost-benefit analysis.
B) MoSCoW prioritization.
C) Change impact analysis.
D) Progressive implementation.
**Correct Answer:** C) Change impact analysis.

**10. Which of the following aspects is evaluated by a change impact analysis?**
A) Supplier contract terms.
B) Project monitoring and reporting metrics.
C) Feelings about the change.
D) The exact timeline of the next project.
**Correct Answer:** C) Feelings about the change.

**11. What is the first step in the change impact analysis process?**
A) Identify organisational areas that will be impacted.
B) Evaluate the level of impact on each area.
C) Define the change, its purpose, objectives, scope and timeline.
D) Develop an action plan to address the impacts.
**Correct Answer:** C) Define the change, its purpose, objectives, scope and timeline.

**12. After defining the change in an impact analysis, what is the next step?**
A) Develop a communication plan.
B) Evaluate the level of financial risk.
C) Identify organisational areas and individual stakeholders that will be impacted.
D) Implement the final project deliverables.
**Correct Answer:** C) Identify organisational areas and individual stakeholders that will be impacted.

**13. What happens in the third step of the change impact analysis process?**
A) The project is formally closed.

C) A shared vision is established.

**Correct Answer:** B) The level of impact that the change will have on each area is evaluated.

**14. An action plan developed during change impact analysis should be aligned with what?**
