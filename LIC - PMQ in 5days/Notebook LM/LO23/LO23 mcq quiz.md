Here are 50 multiple-choice questions based on the provided risk and issue management source material. 

**1. What is a primary benefit of effective risk management in a project?**
A) It eliminates all uncertainties from the project life cycle.
B) It supports more realistic plans, schedules, and budgets.
C) It removes the need for issue management.
D) It bypasses governance requirements.
**Correct Answer:** B. Risk management supports more realistic plans, schedules, and budgets.

**2. What influences the tolerance levels for acceptable risks in an investing organization?**
A) The project manager's personal preference.
B) The organization's risk appetite.
C) The quantitative analysis tools used.
D) The number of issues logged.
**Correct Answer:** B. Risk management is influenced by the risk appetite of the investing organization that will provide tolerance levels.

**3. How is the risk management process described to reflect the dynamic nature of project work?**
A) Linear
B) Static
C) Iterative
D) Reactive
**Correct Answer:** C. The risk management process is iterative to reflect the dynamic nature of project work.

**4. What is the main purpose of the issue management process?**
A) To identify unknown unknowns before they happen.
B) To understand the issue in order to prioritize and assign owners for a timely resolution.
C) To calculate the management reserve.
D) To avoid change control at all costs.
**Correct Answer:** B. The process is focused on understanding the issue to prioritize and assign owners for a timely resolution.

**5. Who supports issue resolution and may escalate issues to the governance board?**
A) The subject matter expert
B) The project sponsor
C) The stakeholders
D) The assurance team
**Correct Answer:** B. Issue resolution requires support from the project sponsor, who may escalate to the governance board.

**6. How is "contingency" defined in project planning?**
A) Hidden extra time to deliver planned scope.
B) Resource set aside for responding to identified and unidentified risks.
C) A budget used only for resolving issues without escalation.
D) Funds used for daily administrative expenses.
**Correct Answer:** B. Contingency is the resource set aside for responding to identified and unidentified risks.

**7. What alternative term is used to describe unidentified risks?**
A) Known unknowns
B) Baseline variables
C) Unknown unknowns
D) Secondary risks
**Correct Answer:** C. Unidentified risks are also known as the ‘unknown unknowns’.

**8. What are the two most typical ways contingency is expressed?**

B) Budget and time
C) Resources and risk appetite
D) Escalation and assurance
**Correct Answer:** B. Contingency is most typically expressed in two ways: budget and time.

**9. In an iterative life cycle using timeboxes, how might a project consider contingency?**

D) By removing the governance board.
**Correct Answer:** B. If using an iterative life cycle and timeboxes, the project considers contingency in terms of scope or quality.

**10. What reserve is specifically kept for unidentified risks or identified risks with very low likelihood but very high impact?**

B) Management reserve
C) Quality reserve
D) Iterative reserve
**Correct Answer:** B. Some projects have access to a management reserve for unidentified risks or risks with low likelihood but high impact.

**11. What must be established first to initiate risk management?**
A) The issue register
B) The risk management plan
C) The change control process
D) The Monte-Carlo simulation
**Correct Answer:** B. Risk management must be initiated by first establishing the risk management plan for the project.

**12. What information does a risk management plan provide?**

B) Roles, responsibilities, risk assessment methods, and agreed tolerances for risk thresholds.
C) The final cost of the management reserve.
D) The exact weather forecast for the project duration.
**Correct Answer:** B. This plan provides the roles and responsibilities, how risks are to be assessed, agreed tolerances, reporting, and escalation points.

**13. What tools and techniques can be used during risk identification?**

B) Timeboxes and iterative deployments.

B) A risk-based approach mindset
C) A qualitative-only mindset
D) A zero-risk mindset
**Correct Answer:** B. Risk identification has a different focus, using a risk-based approach mindset to influence the design of each iteration.

**15. What is the focus of qualitative risk analysis?**

C) Assessing the probability/likelihood of occurrence and the size of impact.

**16. Quantitative analysis is used to determine what?**
