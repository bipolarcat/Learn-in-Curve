# Learn in Curve
## Learning Objective 3: Sustainability

### **Sub-outcome 3a: Understand why sustainability responsibilities, principles and priorities are considered within a project and the impact they may have.**

**1. What is the definition of sustainability in a project-based working environment?**
A) Maximizing profit margins for stakeholders
B) Balancing environmental, social, economic, and administrative aspects to meet current needs without compromising future generations
C) Focusing exclusively on reducing the project's carbon footprint
D) Ensuring all project tasks are completed ahead of schedule
**Correct Answer:** B

**2. When is the most critical time to build sustainability into a project's vision?**
A) During the project's closing phase
B) At project inception
C) When the project goes over budget
D) After the business case is rejected
**Correct Answer:** B

**3. Which four main considerations must be balanced to ensure a project's sustainability?**
A) Internal, External, Financial, Technical
B) Scope, Time, Cost, Quality
C) Environmental, Social, Economic, and Administrative
D) Design, Build, Test, Deploy
**Correct Answer:** C

**4. Climate change, biodiversity, and energy efficiency fall under which sustainability pillar?**
A) Social
B) Economic
C) Environmental
D) Administrative
**Correct Answer:** C

**5. Which of the following is considered a "Social" sustainability impact?**
A) Human rights, equity and equality, and community impact
B) Legislation and regulation
C) Risk management and profitability
D) Energy and resource efficiency
**Correct Answer:** A

**6. Affordability, profit, and risk management are elements of which sustainability consideration?**
A) Environmental
B) Social
C) Economic
D) Administrative
**Correct Answer:** C

**7. Legislation and regulation, health and safety, and resource efficiency are grouped under which sustainability pillar?**
A) Administrative
B) Economic
C) Social
D) Environmental
**Correct Answer:** A

**8. Project sustainability involves responsibility from which of the following parties?**
A) Only individual project managers
B) Only the organisation's executives
C) Both individual and organisational responsibility
D) Only external stakeholders
**Correct Answer:** C

**9. What should be aligned to support the sustainability agenda of an organisation?**
A) The project schedule
B) The business case
C) The daily stand-up meetings
D) The software licenses
**Correct Answer:** B

**10. What does the acronym "SDG" stand for in the context of UN sustainability frameworks?**
A) Sustainable Development Goals
B) Strategic Data Governance
C) Standard Delivery Guidelines
D) Social Diversity Groups
**Correct Answer:** A

**11. What does the acronym "ESG" stand for?**
A) Environmental, Social and Governance
B) Economic, Safety and Governance
C) Equity, Sustainability and Growth
D) Environmental, Social and Growth
**Correct Answer:** A

**12. According to the Learner Study Pack, sustainability initiatives will influence which of the following?**
A) Only the project's end date
B) Product design, manufacturing processes, energy consumption, waste management, and stakeholder relationships
C) Only the software developers' salaries
D) The number of project management offices (PMOs)
**Correct Answer:** B

**13. What cultural shift is recommended for an organisation's internal day-to-day thinking regarding sustainability?**
A) From "we want to do it" to "we are made to do it"
B) From "we are made to do it" to "we want to do it"
C) Ignoring sustainability until requested by the client
D) Making sustainability a purely external marketing tool
**Correct Answer:** B

**14. In "Scenario One," why are the developers at Snappy Appy complaining?**
A) They are underpaid compared to industry standards
B) They have restrictive technology and software impacting their productivity
C) They do not have enough annual leave
D) The office does not have natural lighting

**15. What technical diversity is Snappy Appy aiming to expand into to meet customer demands?**

D) COBOL and Fortran

**16. What does "MOPS" stand for according to Snappy Appy's Head of Development?**

**17. What certification is Snappy Appy's CEO, Ayo, increasingly interested in gaining?**

**18. B-Corporations are recognized for demonstrating high performance in which areas?**
A) Software development speed

**19. In "Scenario Two," which specific 'Best for the World' award is Ayo interested in?**

**20. What is the proposed name of the handbook that will outline staff expectations for sustainable practices at Snappy Appy?**

**21. What is a potential impact of breaching Health & Safety building regulations?**
A) An increase in project budget surplus
B) Financial penalties, injury or death, and legal cases
C) Faster project delivery times
D) Improved community relations
**Correct Answer:** B

**22. If environmental legislations and regulations are not considered on a project, what could be the result?**
A) Better team cohesion
B) Increased operational efficiency
C) Financial penalties, environmental damage, and reputational damage
D) Immediate B-Corp certification

**23. What is the risk of failing to engage the community on a project like upgrading sea defences?**
A) It guarantees the project will finish under budget
B) The community will fund the project instead
C) It could cause bad publicity and issues that risk project success

**24. If the needs of vulnerable or marginalised groups aren't considered on a project, what is the impact?**

B) Increased energy efficiency

**25. Economic costs resulting from a failure to consider sustainability may include which of the following?**

**28. Risk management is a consideration of which sustainability element?**
A) Social

### **Sub-outcome 3b: Knowledge of how sustainability measures are monitored and reported on.**

**31. Organisations should report internally on which of the following sustainability metrics?**
A) Employee browsing histories
B) Carbon emissions, energy consumption, waste management, social impact, and ethical practices
C) Competitors' financial data
D) Unverified social media rumors
**Correct Answer:** B

**32. What are key factors when communicating sustainability performance to external communities?**
A) Secrecy and internal memos
B) Transparency, credibility, and the importance of data and metrics
C) Avoiding data sharing to protect intellectual property
D) Focusing only on future goals without current metrics
**Correct Answer:** B

**33. What should organisations aim for when interacting with suppliers and their supply chain?**
A) Sustainable procurement
B) Short-term cost cutting at all costs
C) Ignoring environmental impact if the price is low
D) Procurement without contracts
**Correct Answer:** A

**34. What does the acronym "GRI" stand for in sustainability reporting?**
A) Global Resource Index
B) General Reporting Instructions
C) Global Reporting Initiative
D) Green Recycling Initiative
**Correct Answer:** C

**35. The Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) provides support for which activities?**
A) Hiring new staff and managing payroll
B) Data collection methods, data analysis techniques, and continuous improvement processes
C) Designing app interfaces
D) Calculating project profit margins exclusively
**Correct Answer:** B

**36. Which of the following is listed as a proactive tool and technique for monitoring sustainability?**
A) ISO 9001
B) ISO 14001
C) PRINCE2
D) Agile Manifesto
**Correct Answer:** B

**37. Along with ISO 14001, which of the following is also a proactive tool for sustainability monitoring?**
A) LEED
B) PMBOK
C) ITIL
D) Six Sigma
**Correct Answer:** A

**38. What does "SASB" stand for as a proactive sustainability reporting tool?**
A) Sustainability Action and Safety Board
B) Sustainable Administration and Sourcing Bureau

D) Social and Sustainability Benchmarking

**39. Measures and metrics for sustainability assessment include which of the following?**

**40. What specific carbon measurements are used to assess sustainability?**
A) Projected and Historical Carbon

B) Only during the execution phase
