# Recruitment Privacy Notice - Learn in Curve

This is the notice UK GDPR expects you to give job applicants specifically -
a separate requirement from the general Privacy Policy, because the data
subject here is a candidate, not a customer. Applies to any role listed in
the site footer.

*Last updated: 30 July 2026*

## What this covers

If you apply for a role at Learn in Curve (currently via emailing
support@learnincurve.com per the Careers section), this notice explains what
happens to the personal data in your application.

## What we collect

Whatever you send us - typically your CV, a cover note, and your email address
and name. We don't currently use an applicant-tracking system or ask for
anything beyond what you choose to include.

## Why, and on what basis

To assess your suitability for the role and contact you about your application -
this is "necessary for steps prior to entering into a contract" under UK GDPR,
which doesn't require your separate consent.

## How long we keep it

We keep applications for 6 months after a role is filled (or after the
application, if the role isn't filled), then delete them - long enough to
reconsider a strong candidate for a similar future role and to defend against
a discrimination claim within the usual time limit, without keeping CVs in an
inbox indefinitely.

## Who sees it

Only whoever's involved in hiring for that specific role - not shared with any
third party, and not used for any automated decision-making.

## Your rights

Same as anyone else's under UK GDPR - access, correction, deletion, etc. See the
main Privacy Policy section 7, or just email support@learnincurve.com.
