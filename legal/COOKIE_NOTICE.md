# Cookie Policy - Learn in Curve
*Last updated: 3 August 2026*

## 1. Introduction
At Learn in Curve ("we", "us"), we believe in being clear and transparent about how we collect and use data related to you. This Cookie Policy applies to learnincurve.com and explains how we use cookies and similar tracking technologies to ensure our platform functions securely and effectively.

This notice is kept separate from our general Privacy Policy to make it easy for you to find and review our specific cookie practices.

## 2. Cookies We Use (Strictly Necessary)
Currently, our platform utilizes only strictly necessary technologies required for the core functioning of the Service. We do not use any non-essential cookies.

- **Authentication Cookie (Supabase):** We use a secure session cookie provided by our backend database infrastructure, Supabase. This cookie is strictly necessary to authenticate your account, securely keep you signed in as you navigate between pages, and remember your login credentials to make accessing the platform easier.
- **Cookie Banner Acknowledgment:** When you view and dismiss our on-site cookie notice, we store a simple preference marker (`lic_cookie_notice_v1`) in your browser's Local Storage. This simply tells our website not to show you the banner again on your next visit. This is a functional preference setting, not a tracking cookie.

Because these technologies are strictly necessary for the service to function and for you to access your account, UK and EU privacy regulations (PECR and GDPR) do not require us to collect explicit prior consent to set them.

## 3. Analytics and Third-Party Cookies
We do not currently load any advertising pixels, third-party marketing trackers, or product analytics tools.

## 4. Future Changes
We are committed to maintaining your privacy as our platform grows. If we decide to introduce product analytics (such as PostHog) or integrate third-party customer support tools in the future, we will update this Cookie Policy accordingly.

Before any non-essential or analytics cookies are ever set on your device, we will update our website’s cookie banner to give you clear, compliant "Accept" and "Reject" choices, as required by law.

## 5. Controlling Cookies Yourself
You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies through your web browser controls. You can set or amend your web browser settings to block or delete cookies at any time.

**Please note:** Because the only cookie we currently use is essential for authentication, blocking or deleting it via your browser settings will immediately sign you out and prevent you from logging into your Learn in Curve account.
