# Cookie Notice - Learn in Curve

Kept separate from the Privacy Policy (section 3 there duplicates this) so it
can be linked directly from a footer "Cookies" link without making people
read the whole privacy policy to find it.

*Last updated: 30 July 2026*

## What we use today

One cookie today: Supabase's authentication session cookie, which keeps you
signed in between visits.

**Intercom Messenger** is wired in code but only boots when its App ID is set
in production. Until then, no Intercom script loads and no Intercom cookies
are set. Once that App ID is added, Intercom may set its own non-essential
cookies for the messenger - this notice and the on-site cookie banner will be
updated before that goes live, not after.

Absent Intercom being live, the Supabase session cookie is still "strictly
necessary" - the site can't keep you signed in without it - so under current
UK/EU rules we don't need a consent *choice* for it alone. We still show a
short on-site cookie notice (paper card, bottom of the screen) so you can
read this page and acknowledge you've seen it. That acknowledgement is stored
in your browser's local storage (`lic_cookie_notice_v1`), not as a tracking
cookie.

## If that changes

If we ever add analytics (even privacy-friendly, cookie-light analytics), turn
on Intercom in production, or add any other non-essential cookie, this page
and the banner will be updated before that cookie is ever set — including
real accept / reject choices where the law requires them.

## Controlling cookies yourself

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; doing so
for the session cookie will just sign you out.
