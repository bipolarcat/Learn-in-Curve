# Should Learn in Curve build a PMP course?

**Written:** 2026-08-10
**Question:** Is a PMP course worth building now, given PMP's larger global market?
**Short answer:** Not yet. The instinct about market size is correct. The timing is wrong, and the reason is competitive, not commercial.

---

## 1. Where the business actually is today

Measured, not estimated. Live `learn-in-curve` database and PostHog:

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total registered users, all time | 23 |
| New users, last 7 days | 21 |
| Free mock exam leads captured | 2 |
| Leads who ticked marketing consent | 0 |
| Library pages indexed by Google | 0 (deployed today) |
| Organic search sessions to date | 0 |

Two things worth reading carefully.

**The last seven days produced 21 of your 23 users.** Something is working. That is the first real signal this business has produced.

**The free mock exam has already captured 2 leads**, within roughly a day of going live, before Google has indexed a single Library page and before any deliberate promotion. Small number, but the mechanism works. Nobody ticked the marketing box, which tells you the consent design is honest and that you should expect a low opt-in rate.

**The relevant fact for this decision:** you have a product that has never had a working acquisition channel, and you turned one on yesterday. You do not yet know what it produces.

---

## 2. The market-size argument is correct

This part of the instinct holds up.

- **1.4 to 1.7 million** active PMP holders worldwide, across 214 countries ([PMI](https://www.pmi.org/), [KnowledgeHut](https://www.knowledgehut.com/blog/project-management/pmp-holders-by-country), [ThinkLouder](https://thinklouder.com/blog/how-many-pmp-certified-professionals-are-there-current-statistics-and-trends/))
- The APM PMQ is a UK-weighted qualification. **APM does not publish annual candidate numbers**, and I could not find a credible figure, so the exact ratio is unknown. Directionally, PMP is unambiguously the larger global market.

So yes: PMP addresses more people, in more countries, with more willingness to pay.

That is not the constraint on this business.

---

## 3. The competitive argument is decisive, and it runs the other way

Your entire strategic advantage in PMQ was that **nobody credible was serving it.**

The first page for "APM PMQ practice questions" is `pass4success`, `marks4sure`, `certkillers`, `p2pexams`, `certshero`, `certempire`. Exam-dump content farms. Ahrefs flags them as spam. Zero real products. That is an unusually weak field, and it is why eleven well-written pages can plausibly win.

Now look at the equivalent PMP field:

| Provider | What they have |
|---|---|
| **PMI Study Hall** | The exam board's own official prep product, 1,000+ official questions |
| PM PrepCast | 2,000+ scenario questions, full exam simulator, analytics |
| MPUG | 350+ questions aligned to the current Exam Content Outline |
| PMTI, Mometrix, PMlearning, ExamCert, Whizlabs, Simplilearn | Established prep businesses with years of content and backlinks |
| Oliver Lehmann | Long-standing recognised name in PMP preparation |

Sources: [PrepCast](https://www.project-management-prepcast.com/pmp-practice-exam-questions-sample-test), [MPUG](https://mpug.com/pmp-practice-exam), [PMTI](https://www.4pmti.com/learn/free-pmp-practice-questions/), [ExamCert](https://www.examcert.app/blog/free-pmp-practice-questions-2026/)

**You would be competing against the organisation that sets the exam.** PMI sells its own official study product. That is a competitor you cannot out-credential.

Your Domain Rating is 0. You have zero indexed content pages and no organic traffic. Entering a market with a dozen funded incumbents and the awarding body itself, from that starting position, with no proven distribution, is the hardest possible version of this.

**The asymmetry in one line:** PMQ is a weak SERP you can win with quality. PMP is a strong SERP where quality is table stakes and everyone already has it.

---

## 4. Content cost and live risk

**The PMP exam changed on 9 July 2026, roughly a month ago.**

Two consequences.

**Sources currently disagree with each other.** Searching now returns inconsistent figures on question count, duration and domain weightings, with some pages still quoting the 2021 outline. Anything built from secondary sources today carries a real risk of being wrong.

**Roughly half the exam covers agile and hybrid approaches**, woven through all three domains. None of your PMQ content transfers to that. This is not an adaptation of what you have; it is a new build from zero.

Add the ongoing burden: PMP content must track PMI's Exam Content Outline, and you would be maintaining two full syllabi with two independent update cycles, alone.

**One legal note.** The same rules that apply to APM apply to PMI. "PMP" and "PMI" are PMI trademarks, usable nominatively to describe what you prepare people for, never in a way implying endorsement or accreditation. Questions must be original, written from the published outline. PMI is a larger organisation with more active brand enforcement than APM. This needs solicitor review before any PMP-branded launch, not after.

---

## 5. The failure mode this would be

Building a second product because the first one is not selling is the most common first-time-founder error, and it is worth naming plainly.

You have 23 users. That is not a product problem. You have a well-built product with 2,022 questions, four mock exams, an AI tutor and a five-day structure. **It has never had a functioning acquisition channel until yesterday.**

A PMP course does not fix distribution. It doubles the content surface, splits your attention, and delays the only thing that has ever been the actual constraint.

The market-size logic assumes distribution is solved and the addressable market is the limit. Neither is true yet.

---

## 6. What the evidence supports instead

**Keep the PMP page you already have.** `/library/apm-pmq-vs-pmp` is live. It captures PMP-curious search traffic at zero additional cost, and it is honest, because it sends people to whichever qualification suits them. If PMP demand is real in your audience, that page will show it in Search Console impressions within weeks. That is a free market test.

**Write two or three more PMP-adjacent Library pages**, not a course. "Is the PMP worth it in the UK", "PMP eligibility requirements explained". Cheap, reuses the model that works, and generates demand evidence before you commit build time.

**Spend the next 90 days proving the PMQ channel.** You now have instrumentation, a lead magnet and eleven indexed pages arriving. You are weeks away from knowing whether this model works at all. Find out before duplicating it.

---

## 7. The decision rule

Build the PMP course when **all** of these are true. Not before, and not on a hunch.

1. **Organic search delivers 250+ sessions/week** to the Library, proving the content model works
2. **The free mock converts at 30%+** to email, proving the funnel works
3. **PMQ signups compound month on month** from a channel you can name in Search Console
4. **Search Console shows real PMP impressions** on your existing comparison page, proving the demand is in *your* audience
5. **PMI's post-July-2026 outline is stable** and verified against PMI directly, not blog posts

Realistically that is a Q4 2026 conversation, not an August one.

**The stronger intermediate move, if you want a second product sooner:** a free PFQ practice test as a lead magnet into PMQ. Same market, same syllabus family, no new content universe, no new trademark exposure, and it feeds the product you already have.

---

## 8. What I do not know

Stated so the report is not read as more certain than it is.

- **APM does not publish PMQ candidate volumes.** The PMQ-versus-PMP market ratio is directional, not measured.
- **No keyword volume data.** Ahrefs Keywords Explorer is paid and Bing Webmaster Tools has not been set up. The competitive read comes from reading live SERPs, which is solid; the demand read is inferred.
- **PMP exam specifics are genuinely unstable right now** following the July 2026 change. Verify with PMI directly before acting on any figure, including the ones in this document.
- **Your acquisition channel is one day old.** Every conclusion here should be revisited once real Search Console data exists. If the Library massively outperforms expectations, the timeline compresses.

---

## Bottom line

The reasoning about global market size is right, and a PMP course is very likely the correct second product **eventually**.

It is the wrong thing to build now, because your advantage in PMQ came from a weak competitive field, and that advantage does not exist in PMP. You would be trading a market you can win for a bigger one you probably cannot, before you have proven you can win anything.

Prove the channel on the qualification where the field is weak. Then take that working machine to the bigger market.
