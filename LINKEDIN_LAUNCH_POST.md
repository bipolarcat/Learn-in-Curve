I've spent the last few weeks building something I wished existed when I was
studying for my APM PMQ exam: a revision platform that doesn't feel like a
374-page PDF.

Today I'm launching **Learn in Curve** 🎉

It's built around "PMQ in 5 days" — bite-sized lessons, quizzes with instant
feedback, XP and streaks to keep you moving, and a full mock exam. It's free
to use, and it's staying free. No paywall on the content that actually gets
you exam-ready.

An AI tutor is coming soon — a study companion that answers questions on
exactly what you're revising and points out your weak spots based on your own
quiz history. It's not live yet, so for now you'll see it marked "Coming
soon" on every lesson page. I'd rather ship something real and add to it than
launch a feature that isn't ready.

This is v1. It's a solo build, it'll have rough edges, and I'd genuinely love
feedback from anyone prepping for PMQ (or anyone who's sat through a
certification exam and has opinions on how revision tools should work).
