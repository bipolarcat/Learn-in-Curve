# Roadmap — Learn in Curve platform

Supersedes the original single-course roadmap. Learn in Curve is now a multi-course exam-revision SaaS platform, not a single PMQ tutor. "PMQ in 5 days" (live, v1.7, ~60 logins, free/basic) becomes the first course inside it. See `BUSINESS_STATE.md` for the decision log and current status.

## Product thesis

Not a general PM learning platform — a gamified, interactive, exam-focused revision tool. The pitch: pay a small one-time fee (~£10) per course for an interactive experience, rather than a fiver for a static PDF. AI tutor and content are scoped strictly to "help pass this specific exam," not general subject mastery.
