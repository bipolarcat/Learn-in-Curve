# BoK 8e source map — syllabus → source locations

Built from the Body of Knowledge 8th edition contents and back-of-book index, cross-checked
against the PMQ 2024 Handbook syllabus.

**Purpose:** make per-LO reads *complete* rather than guesswork. Before authoring any LO, read
every location listed for it — not just the obviously-named chapter. LO1 proved the failure mode:
the most directly examinable content (why projects are structured as phases) sits in chapter 1,
sixty pages before the chapter called "Project life cycles".

Page numbers are BoK 8e printed page numbers.

### What this means

LO12 and LO13 sit in competence area C, which is **25–35% of the exam**. LO4 sits in area A.
For those learning objectives the two permitted sources are **not sufficient** — the syllabus
requires knowledge of models the Body of Knowledge does not teach.

This is a source gap, not a tooling gap. No extraction method fixes it, and any tool asked to
produce LO12 or LO13 content from these two sources alone will either omit the named models or
invent coverage of them.

**Resolution options:**

1. **Write those models from general knowledge.** Maslow, Herzberg, McGregor, Belbin, Tuckman,
   Myers-Briggs, Katzenbach & Smith, SWOT and PESTLE are long-established, widely documented
   management concepts. They are not APM intellectual property and there is no copyright
   obstacle to explaining them in our own words. Lowest friction, and legitimate.
2. **Add a permitted third source** for those specific models, recorded in `SOURCE_REGISTER.md`.
3. **Cover them lightly** and accept the risk. Not recommended — these are explicitly named in
   the syllabus and sit in the most heavily weighted competence area.

Recommendation: option 1, with each such model flagged in its LO file as
`"source": "general management knowledge — not in BoK 8e"` so the provenance stays honest and
auditable.
