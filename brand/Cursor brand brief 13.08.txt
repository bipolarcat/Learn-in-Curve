Learn in Curve — Brand / Design Brief (live)
One-line philosophy
Warm paper + stamp / transit-ticket product design: cream dotted paper, Fraunces headlines, Figtree UI, orange primary action, flat illustrated props — not generic SaaS purple, not dark-mode-first, not photoreal.

Voice of the system: calm study tool with personality; color lives inside tickets, stamps, and frames — not as stacked full-bleed section bands.

Rules

Default surface = cream paper + soft ink.
Orange is the hero accent (often one orange word in a Fraunces headline).
Don’t introduce purple/indigo gradients, stark white SaaS panels, or neon glow.
Dark mode exists only on specific product routes; marketing is light-first.

Patterns

Brand lockup: “Learn in” + orange “Curve”.
Headlines: tight tracking (~-0.025em), display weight, balanced line length.
UI copy: sentence case, not ALL CAPS stamp labels.
Fallbacks: Fraunces → Georgia; Figtree → system-ui.
Surfaces & texture
Dot grid on cream (radial-gradient ink dots, low opacity) — default marketing/dashboard ground.
Film grain overlay sitewide (~5%, multiply) — subtle, not grunge.
Paper cards: paper fill, thin ink/8–12% border, soft shadow (1px + larger soft ink shadow), rounded-xl (~12–20px). Prefer soft modern elevation over heavy “sticker” offsets on product UI.
Marketing motif still allows ticket language (perforations, dashed dividers, slight rotate) for course/marketing cards — not for every form control.
Components / motifs to reuse
Stamp CTAs: solid orange (or ink) pill/chip, Figtree, optional arrow — primary enrol actions.
Floating glass header: paper + blur, thin border, soft rounded bar; brand mark + fox logo.
Soft header pills: flat fills, 150ms press, no glow shadows.
Quiet field errors: Figtree, muted ink (~55%), not loud red banners.
Success = olive; wrong = rust (quiz feedback).
Mascot: Sly the fox + flat illustrated props (plane, mailbox, paper airplane) — illustration, not photo/3D.
Footer: ink band + cream “Keep Learning” flickering grid treatment.
Motion principles
Motion happens to objects, never to layout (no type sliding in from the side, no grid reflow as animation).
Idle motion: slow and small.
Interaction: snappy (~150ms), ease-out-quint / expo curves.
Prefer Framer Motion; respect prefers-reduced-motion → final pose, no sticky scrub.
One calm entrance beat > scattered micro-interactions.
Layout / composition rules
Continuous cream dotted paper scroll for marketing surfaces.
Color inside frames/tickets/stamps — avoid full-bleed colored section bands.
Section gaps generous (clamp(~5rem, 10vw, 8rem) on home).
One primary CTA job per section.
Cards only when they hold interaction or a distinct framed unit; don’t card everything.
Brand must be readable without the nav: Fraunces wordmark + orange “Curve” + fox mark.
What not to do
Generic AI SaaS look (purple gradients, Inter/Roboto, white cards on grey).
Photoreal / 3D replacing flat illustration.
Pre-ticked “marketing” visual patterns that feel templatey: pill clusters, stat strips, floating badges on heroes.
Confetti/badges as the product identity — journey + quiz feedback matter more.
Space Mono / uppercase stamp labels (retired on live site).
Copy / tone cues that match the visual
Warm, direct, exam-focused. Orange for emphasis words. Quiet helper text in muted ink. No hype-bro SaaS voice.
