Why projects are structured as phases Unchanged

The syllabus asks for this in so many words, so learn it as three named reasons.

ReasonWhat it buys you
Better grouping of activitiesPhases group work by type, analysis, planning, building, so the right people are on the right kind of work at the right time.
More accurate plans and estimatesSmaller chunks are easier to plan and estimate, which raises both accuracy and confidence.
Tracking and monitoring progressPhases create clear break points to measure against checkpoints and milestones, so you can see early whether things are on track.
