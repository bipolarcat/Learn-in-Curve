## Vision

Studying for a project management exam shouldn't feel like a chore. We want revision to feel simple, honest, and actually doable — something you can fit into a lunch break or a commute and still feel like it's working.

## Goal

Our goal is simple: help you walk into your PMQ exam feeling properly ready, not just like you've scrolled through a lot of slides. Everything here is built around one thing — getting you to pass.

## Values

We value honesty over polish, usefulness over fluff, and care over scale. That means saying plainly what's free and what isn't, building the thing that actually helps you pass, and never dressing up a weak product with hype — even when the flashy version would be easier to ship.

## Founder

Learn in Curve was built by one person — a working project manager named Sim, who's spent years managing real projects and real deadlines, and who got curious about what AI could do to make studying less painful. This isn't a big company. It's a genuine attempt to build the thing he wished someone had built for him — made with a lot of care, and a bit of stubbornness.
