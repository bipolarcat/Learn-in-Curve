What I actually did

24 objectives.
24 notebooks.

Learn in Curve has 24 learning objectives. I needed quiz questions for each.

The obvious move is one notebook with everything in it. That's the wrong move.

NotebookLM's strength is that it stays inside its sources. So I split them — one per objective, each told: this is the source of truth, do not go off topic.

Cross-contamination between objectives never happened, because structurally it couldn't.

The thing nobody says

You're not choosing
between two tools.

You're choosing
between two bundles.

£18.99 buys a notebook, an image model, a video tool, an agentic dev platform and 5TB of storage.

$20 buys depth on one workflow.

Neither is wrong.

They're different bets — and most comparisons never mention that the unit of comparison isn't the tool.

Disclosure: I have both through Lenny's Product Pass, so I haven't felt the monthly cost the way a normal subscriber would.
